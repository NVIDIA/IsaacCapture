Hand Suite#

Preview

The hand tolerances are provisional and not yet calibrated against glove recordings, so treat a result close to a limit as indicative.

Run this suite if your device publishes hand poses: a glove through its plugin, or a headset’s own optical hand tracking. You record one take of eleven scripted gestures; the checker reads the recording and its labels and returns a verdict.

At a glance#

Data

core.HandPoseRecord, left and right hand channels

Joints

26 OpenXR joints per hand, 21 judged. PALM and the four non-thumb metacarpals are optional, so a 21-joint glove can pass.

Script

11 steps, seated at a table. About 2 minutes, paced by a timer: you press only Start recording.

Checks

40, in four groups: signal, hand shape, placement, and the scripted steps

Your plugin

Injects both hands with plugin_utils::HandInjector: the suite records what Isaac Capture’s OpenXR hand tracker reads, not a channel of your own. Joints in metres, placed in the room, at 30 Hz or more. A glove with no wrist pose takes one from headset hand tracking or a controller (plugin_utils::WristPoseSource).

Finish Set Up the Tools first: tool environments, your plugin installed, and the headset connected through CloudXR.

How each step runs#

  • Audio is optional. The capture panel shows each cue as text, with the count. To hear the cues, connect speakers to the workstation; they do not play in the headset. Without speakers, keep the capture panel in the performer’s view or have the helper read each cue aloud; the beep is the moment the panel switches to hold.

  • Be in the pose by the beep, then keep still. Move during the cue and count-in.

  • The first 40% of each hold is ignored, so a slightly late arrival is fine.

  • The two rolls are measured whole, from beep to tick. The cue’s “at the tone” means the beep, not the count-in: start turning then, at least 90° out, then back.

Every step is also checked for the gross motion its name asks for (segmentation.labelled_step_actually_performed); a missed step gives retake.

The eleven steps#

Both hands perform every step together, except the two rolls.

1 · Flat on the table

hold 4 s “Rest both hands flat on the table, palms down, fingers together.”

Fingers straight and together, pressed onto the table. Do not hover.

Checked: fingers straight (posture.flat_hand_extension), joints in one plane (table.fingers_coplanar), level (table.level), both hands at one height (table.hands_same_height).

2 · Fists

hold 4 s “Make two tight fists.”

Curl every finger fully, thumb wrapped over the fingers.

Checked: fingers curled (posture.fist_closure), more than in the flat steps (segmentation.step_order_matches_labels).

3 · Pinch: index

hold 3 s “Thumb to index fingertip.”

Pad to pad on both hands, the other fingers relaxed and open.

Checked: the tip nearest the thumb is the index (pinch.finger_identity). Measured: thumb-to-tip distance (pinch.contact_distance).

4 · Pinch: middle

hold 3 s “Thumb to middle fingertip.”

Pad to pad on both hands, the other fingers relaxed and open.

Checked: the tip nearest the thumb is the middle (pinch.finger_identity). Measured: thumb-to-tip distance (pinch.contact_distance).

5 · Pinch: ring

hold 3 s “Thumb to ring fingertip.”

Pad to pad on both hands, the other fingers relaxed and open.

Checked: the tip nearest the thumb is the ring (pinch.finger_identity). Measured: thumb-to-tip distance (pinch.contact_distance).

6 · Pinch: little

hold 3 s “Thumb to little fingertip.”

Pad to pad on both hands, the other fingers relaxed and open.

Checked: the tip nearest the thumb is the little (pinch.finger_identity). Measured: thumb-to-tip distance (pinch.contact_distance).

7 · Palms together

hold 4 s “Press your palms together, fingers up.”

Palms flat against each other, fingers up, thumbs closed alongside the index.

Checked: each fingertip sits opposite its namesake on the other hand, within 5 cm (palms.fingertip_gap).

8 · Fingertips together

hold 3 s “Raise both hands, palms facing you. Touch middle fingertips.”

Fingers point at each other; only the two middle fingertips touch.

Checked: the middle tips touch; this is the still reference for the rolls (tips.roll_contact).

9 · Right tip roll

turn 6 s “Fingertips touching. At the tone, turn the right palm out, then back.”

Left hand still. At the beep, turn the right palm out at least 90° and back, tips touching.

Checked: the turning tip stays on the still one (tips.roll_contact); the wrist turns with the joints (consistency.position_orientation_same_frame).

10 · Left tip roll

turn 6 s “Fingertips touching. At the tone, turn the left palm out, then back.”

Right hand still. At the beep, turn the left palm out at least 90° and back, tips touching.

Checked: the turning tip stays on the still one (tips.roll_contact); the wrist turns with the joints (consistency.position_orientation_same_frame).

11 · Flat on the table again

hold 4 s “Hands flat on the table again.”

Same as step 1: fingers straight and together, pressed onto the table.

Checked: the four step 1 checks. Measured: shape and height drift since step 1 (drift.flat_hand_between_windows).

A roll under 90°, or middle tips more than 8 cm apart in step 8, gives retake. How often each of the 26 joints is valid is measured (coverage.all_26_valid_rate), not required.

Rehearse#

Download hand-rehearsal.mp3 (about 2 minutes). It plays the cues, counts, beeps and ticks at the real timing. Perform along with it before your first take.

  • Press and touch for real: hands on the table, palms together, fingertips meeting.

  • You cannot pause or skip. If a step goes wrong, let it finish and record again.

  • Practice takes cost nothing. Takes are never overwritten; submit your best one.

  • Have a helper watch the capture panel while you perform.

Record#

Run record.sh from the repository root. The first argument is required and names your device: free text that names the take directory and files and is stored as device in the provenance. The performer wears the connected headset; for headset hands, or a glove wrist from hand tracking, it must see the hands.

Headset optical hands, no plugin:

src/python/isaaccapture/cts/hand/capture/record.sh quest3

A glove, through its plugin. glovex and glove_x_plugin are placeholders for a fictional Glove X: use your device’s name and the name: from your plugin’s plugin.yaml. The plugin is loaded from install/plugins/ (Set Up the Tools), and one that fails to load stops the take. Environment variables your plugin reads, such as where it takes the wrist pose from, go on the same command line.

src/python/isaaccapture/cts/hand/capture/record.sh glovex --plugin glove_x_plugin

--note TEXT, --plugin-version NAME=VERSION and --wrist-source SOURCE (where a glove’s wrist pose comes from, such as hand_tracking or controller) are stored with the take. --wrist-source defaults to the plugin’s own variable, named after it: GLOVE_X_WRIST_SOURCE for glove_x_plugin (upper-cased, without _plugin). If your plugin reads its wrist source from an environment variable, name it that way and takes are labelled without the flag. Then:

  1. Open the capture panel at the address the run prints: http://localhost:8081 unless that port was taken, or the [capture] headset: address in the headset’s browser.

  2. Press Start recording. The script starts once both hands have had a valid joint. A red No hand data after 10 s box lists causes; the take keeps running.

  3. Perform the eleven steps. After “Done. You can stop now.” the capture panel writes the labels and audits them, row by row, ok or BAD:

    • BAD on hand channels or segmentation.label_alignment: one hand sent no records, or the step windows have no frames. Look at the data stream.

    • BAD on segmentation.labelled_step_actually_performed: a step was missed (the detail names it). Record another take.

  4. Press Ctrl+C when you have read it.

~/isaaccapture-cts/glovex_2026-03-04_101530/
├── glovex_2026-03-04_101530-hand.mcap          the recording
├── glovex_2026-03-04_101530-hand.labels.json   one window per step
├── glovex_2026-03-04_101530-hand.json          provenance: device, plugins, commit
└── glovex_2026-03-04_101530-hand.log           capture panel output and label audit

Keep the four files together and never rename them. The labels are stamped during the take and cannot be regenerated.

Check#

Point the checker at the .mcap inside its take directory, so it finds the labels file beside it (to check on another machine, copy the whole directory):

src/python/isaaccapture/cts/hand/checker/.venv/bin/python -m hand_cts.cli \
    ~/isaaccapture-cts/<take>/<take>-hand.mcap

It prints the verdict and one line per check, and exits 0 pass, 1 fail, 2 retake or 3 insufficient_data, or 4 when the check could not run; Verdicts says what each means and what to do. Each [FAIL] line names the check and what it measured; hand/checker/README.md explains every check. The output for a synthetic reference take, shortened:

source   hand/oracle/fixtures/golden_full_script.mcap
frames   8100
topic    hands/left_hand  schema core.HandPoseRecord
verdict  PASS

  …
  [pass] rate.minimum_rate                      left: 60.0 Hz; right: 60.0 Hz
  [pass] rate.frame_gaps                        left: no gaps; right: no gaps
  [pass] continuity.max_joint_velocity          left: peak 1.5 m/s; right: peak 1.5 m/s
  …
  [pass] posture.fist_closure                   left: straightness 0.39; right: straightness 0.39
  …
  [pass] palms.fingertip_gap                    index 18 mm, middle 22 mm, ring 18 mm, little 10 mm
  …

If the report says no motion-step labels beside the recording, fix the path before trusting a pass. --json prints the machine-readable report, --list-checks lists every check without a recording, and --check NAME runs only that check.

View#

The viewer needs the checker environment set up with --panel (Set Up the Tools):

src/python/isaaccapture/cts/hand/checker/.venv/bin/python -m hand_cts.panel \
    ~/isaaccapture-cts/<take>/<take>-hand.mcap

It prints the same report and the address it serves, http://127.0.0.1:8080 by default (--host 0.0.0.0 to reach it from another machine). The viewer replays both hands with the current step and lists the checks, the deciding ones first; invalid joints show red. Its package for submission button builds the archive you send: see Submit Your Results.

Troubleshooting#

What you see

What to do

Start recording hangs after waiting for a system... in the terminal

No headset is connected to CloudXR; connect it (Set Up the Tools) and the take goes on. Ctrl+C does not work here; close the terminal to get out.

No hand data after 10 s

Plugin: it exited, the glove is off, unpaired or uncalibrated, or no wrist source is tracked. Headset: hand tracking is off or the hands are out of view.

The capture panel shows 21 / 26 valid in red

Expected for a 21-joint glove; only the 21 required joints are judged.

The capture panel says Spoken cues are off

aplay is missing. Install alsa-utils and restart the panel to hear the cues, or record with the on-screen cues only.

… is a Git LFS pointer, not audio at startup

Install git-lfs and run git lfs pull.

the session did not open: … Required plugin … was not discovered, or --plugin given but no plugins/ or install/plugins/ in the repo

--plugin must match the name: in an installed plugin.yaml. Run cmake --install build (Set Up the Tools), then record again.

More in hand/README.md (recording) and hand/checker/README.md (every check and the report format).
