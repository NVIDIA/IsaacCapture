XR headsets
===========

XR headsets provide head poses and controller or hand input.

The :doc:`/teleop/quick_start` covers setup and connection for Meta Quest,
PICO, and Apple Vision Pro. Quest and PICO use the WebXR client; Apple Vision
Pro uses the native sample client. See :doc:`/device/trackers/index` for the
tracking interfaces and :doc:`/device/body_tracking/index` for body tracking.
